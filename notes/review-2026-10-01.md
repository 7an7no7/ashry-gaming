## Part 2 - improvements per game (the strongest; full reasons in the reviewers' notes)

**Missing endings / podiums** (motion, mostly S): ارسم وخمّن never ends (Owner?), كلمة واحدة rooms never end, room القنبلة never ends (Owner?), ربع قرد, الكراسي, قبل ولا بعد TV, العقل, الجرس «خلّصنا», الأوضة المضلمة stars, ارسم واكتب vote, battleship tally, bank count-up, timed solo results don't count up.

**Rules worth asking the owner**
- الجاسوس rooms: first asker + optional discussion limit + show the vote bars.
- الفنان المزيف: show the fake the category (needs tagged draw words).
- العقل: level cap by player count (12/10/8) - today almost unwinnable.
- الخزنة «من الوقت»: guessing beats reading; growing penalty.
- المهمة السرية: feminine wording (all 140 missions say يقول/يجيبلك); «كشفتك!» too sure at 3-4 people.
- الحقوا!: mashing controls beats listening.
- الشاهد: witness can just name the suspect.
- المشنقة: unsolvable word pays the writer most.
- خمّن مين / المخ والإيد: a mis-tapped answer/call can't be taken back.
- Duels: forced last move (X-O, Connect 4) not in `ROOM_FORCED_GAMES`; quiet seated phone stalls.
- شطرنج الأربعة: humans out wait ~10 min for bots; first move has no clock; 24 px squares at 375.
