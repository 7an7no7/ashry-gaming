# الخزنة («صندوق جدّو», id `vault`)

Built 1 Oct 2026. The owner's rules as decided, what was decided while building, and how it works.

## The owner's spec

- **Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes, rebuilt our way.** Every safe is generated by the app
  (seeded): no content list.
- **Three ways to play, the host picks in the lobby:**
  1. **«واحد بيفتح»**: one phone (or the TV, if the host chooses the TV as the opener) shows
     the safe; every other phone has a *different* page of the manual (the pages split between
     the readers; with fewer readers than pages a reader holds more than one), so they must
     talk. The opener rotates every safe.
  2. **«الكل»**: every phone shows one part (lock) of the safe *and* a manual page that helps
     someone *else's* lock; everyone talks at once.
  3. **«فريقين»**: two teams, the same safe for both, each team with its own opener and its
     readers; the first team to open it wins the safe.
- **The four locks, all of them in the game:** الأسلاك (3-6 coloured wires; the manual has rules
  by the count and the colours, «لو في سلكين أحمر اقطع الأخير»), الرموز (four strange symbols to
  press in the order of the manual's one column that holds all four, top to bottom; symbols that
  can only be described, drawn as SVG), القرص (a dial showing a shape; the manual turns it into a
  3-number code), النور (lights flash a colour pattern; the manual maps each colour to the button
  to press, and the map changes with the number of mistakes so far).
- **Mistakes, the host picks:** «٣ غلطات» (each strike makes the clock run faster, the third
  sets off the alarm and the safe is lost) or «من الوقت» (each mistake takes time off: 15 s, then 30, 45…).
- **Winning, a lobby switch:** endless levels (each safe harder, less time; the room's best kept
  for the evening; co-op, nothing on the night's board) or a fixed set of safes (3 / 5 / 7) with
  points, banked on the night's points.
- **Hidden information on the server:** a reader's phone gets only its page(s); the opener's only
  the locks' look, never the solution; the TV never the solutions or the pages' contents.
- **The look:** look ب «صندوق جدّو» of the sheet `khazna.html`
  (https://claude.ai/artifact/H29wrVGBJAFMFGhUiAm6Td): an old wooden chest, one lock at a time
  shown big with four brass padlocks along the top and swipe/arrows between them (it moves to the
  next by itself after one opens), the time a melting candle, mistakes chalk ✗ marks; the manual
  grandpa's lined notebook with doodles; the TV the attic, the pages hanging on a washing line with
  each holder's name, the chest's lid opening with a glow.

## Decided while building (open to change, each in one place)

All the numbers are in `Vault.js` (`vaultLevel` and the `VAULT_*` constants).

- **Players:** 2-10 (an opener and a reader at least; «فريقين» 4); the eleventh on watches. A
  latecomer watches and is dealt in by play again.
- **Locks per safe** («واحد بيفتح», «فريقين»): 2 at level 1, 3 at level 2, all 4 from level 3
  (which ones: random, shown in the fixed order wires, symbols, dial, lights). «الكل»: one lock per
  phone (the four kinds in a shuffled cycle, so 5 phones have two of one kind, each its own look).
- **Harder variants:** 3-4 wires at levels 1-2, 3-5 at 3-4, 4-6 from 5; 4 symbols, 5 from level 5;
  a light pattern of 3, 4 from level 3, 5 from level 5.
- **The candle (time per safe):** 2:30 at level 1, 3:00 at level 2, 3:30 at level 3, then 15 s less
  a level, never under 2:00. «الكل» (every lock at once): 2:30, 10 s less a level, never under 1:15.
  A set of safes plays safe k at level k.
- **Strikes:** the candle burns ×1.25 after the first, ×1.5 after the second; the third is the
  alarm (`VAULT_SPEEDUP` 0.25). «من الوقت»: a safe's first mistake −15 s, the second −30, the third
  −45… (`vaultPenaltyMs(n)` = `VAULT_PENALTY_MS` × n, back to 15 at every safe: the review of 1 Oct
  2026 found a flat 15 s made guessing cheaper than reading), no speed-up, the safe lost when the
  candle is out. The opener's phone says what the next one costs («الغلطة الجاية بتحرق 30 ثانية»),
  the TV beside its mistakes count («الجاية −30 ث»), and the `mistake` event carries `pen` (seconds:
  the stamp and the TV's line say −15, −30…; an older page's event without it reads 15). In «فريقين» each team has its own candle and its own mistakes.
- **The safe's card:** 5 s before the candle is lit (the locks and pages are already there to
  read; nothing can be worked yet). A safe's result stays 6 s, then the next comes by itself (the
  host, or anyone once the host is away, can move on sooner: `nextSafe`, a move-on action).
- **Endless:** a lost safe ends the game; the safes opened are the score; the room's best is kept
  for the evening (`room._vaultBest`, across play again and trips to the hub). Co-op: no board,
  nothing on the night.
- **«فريقين» is always a set** (a race is scored); the teams are drawn at random (shuffled, split
  alternately) at every start and play again. When one team's alarm goes off, the other plays on;
  the first to open wins the safe; both out: the safe is lost for both.
- **Points (a set):** a safe opened, «واحد بيفتح» / «فريقين» (the winning team only): the opener 3,
  each reader 2, and +1 to everyone on that side when it opened with no mistake. «الكل»: a lock
  opened 2 to its holder (+1 if nobody slipped on it, +1 for the first lock of the safe to open),
  1 to whoever held its page, and the same +1 for a clean safe. A lost safe scores nothing. The
  board banks the night's points (5 / 3 / 2 / 1) through `bankNightPoints` like every scored game.
- **The notebook is generated once a game** (its seed kept by the server), so a table can learn
  its pages over a game but not across games: per wire count three rules from templates (no X,
  the last is X, the first is X, more than one X, exactly one X) and «غير كده»; six columns of
  seven symbols drawn from 32; a 3-digit code for each of 9 shapes (no code a palindrome or
  another's reverse) and one twist from the chest's number (its last digit odd / its first digit
  even / its digits adding to 20 or more → read the code backwards); three light rows (no
  mistakes, one, two or more), each a derangement of red, blue, green, yellow.
- **The chest's number** (4 digits, a new one each safe) is public: on the chest, the TV and
  «الكل»'s phones; a reader in «واحد بيفتح» is told to ask the opener for it.
- **Pages:** a lock's page whole, or in halves when the readers outnumber the locks (wires 3-4 /
  5-6, symbols columns 1-3 / 4-6, dial shapes 1-5 / 6-9 with the twist on both, lights red+blue /
  green+yellow), shared out contiguously; with more readers than half pages, copies go round. A
  team or a table left with one person gives it the locks and the whole notebook.
- **A wire is cut in two taps** (the first arms it, ✂ shows; the second cuts): a mis-tap on a
  cutter is not a strike. Symbols, lights and the dial's «لِف وافتح» are single taps (the
  dial's digits are set freely before).
- **The TV as opener** needs a screen in the room when the game starts; its looks go to the
  screens only (`room.screenOnly`). If the TV goes, the server's next look hands the safe to the
  first phone, progress kept: a screen leaving never reaches `vaultPlayerLeft`, so
  `vaultDeadline` asks for a look at once while `tvOpens` has no screen (`vaultTvGone`) and
  `vaultTimeout` does the handover (the review of 1 Oct 2026: nobody could open the safe until
  the candle burnt out). The host's «📱 خلّي موبايل يفتح» (`takeOver { safe }`) does it by hand,
  after a confirm (`vt_take_over_confirm`). The chest number on the phone's foot is `.metric`.
- **A leaver:** their locks and pages are dealt again to who is left on their side (a lock's
  progress stays with the lock); a side with nobody, or a table of one, ends the game ('left').

## How it is built

Game id `vault` everywhere (`room-vault`, `ROOM_GAMES.vault`, `TV_GAMES.vault`, the catalog, the
help); the rules are named `vault` / `VAULT_`, the page's code `vt` / `VT_`, the stylesheet
section 63 (`.vt-*`). Accent amber; the party section; a drawn icon (`art:vault`: the chest with a
brass padlock).

- **`Vault.js`** (shared, no DOM; inlined into the page through `SHARED_LISTS`, bundled into the
  Worker before `RoomGames.js`): the numbers, `vaultLevel`, the colours, the 32 symbols
  (`VAULT_SYMBOLS`: SVG paths) and 9 shapes, the seeded generator (`vaultRng`: mulberry32),
  `vaultManual(seed)`, `vaultMakeSafe(seed, manual, kinds, L)`, the answers
  (`vaultWireAnswer`, `vaultSymbolOrder`, `vaultDialCode` / `vaultTwistOn`, `vaultLightAnswer`),
  and the pages (`vaultUnits`, `vaultDealUnits`, `vaultPageData`).
- **`RoomVault.js`** (bundled after `RoomWire.js`): `room._vault = { seed, manual, safe, prog,
  first }`, never projected. `shared`: `settings { way, mistakes, win, count, opener }`, `way`,
  `roster`, `alive`, `teams`, `tvOpens`, `safeNo`, `level`, `total`, `levelsWon`, `best`,
  `newBest`, `scores`, `board` (a set), `serial`, `locks [{ i, k }]`, `sides { x | a, b: { ids,
  opener, strikes, mistakes, left, at, rate, ms, open [], done, why, holders { pid: { locks [],
  pages [] } } } }`, `result`, `events` (the last 30: `safe`, `open`, `step`, `mistake`, `lost`,
  `safeOpen`, `safeLost`, `over`), `phase` ('ready' → 'play' → 'result' → … → 'gameover'),
  `startAt`, `nextAt`. A candle is `left` ms at `at`, burning at `rate` (0 once its side is done).
  Each holder's slice (`vaultWrite`): `{ side, safe, locks: [{ i, k, look, prog }], pages:
  [vaultPageData] }`. Moves: `cut { i, w, safe }`, `sym { i, s }`, `dial { i, code }`, `light
  { i, c }` (from the holder of lock `i`, or a screen when the TV opens; a stale `safe` dropped),
  the host's `start` / `playAgain`, and the move-ons `nextSafe { safe }` and `takeOver { safe }` (a stale `safe` dropped; optional).
  `vaultTimeout` lights the candles and goes on to the burnt-out check in the same pass.
- **`JS_RoomVault.html`**: the phone (`vtPhoneHtml`): the top (the candle `vtCandleHtml` and its
  clock, the hasp of padlocks `vtPadSvg`, a team ribbon), the stage (`vtStageHtml`: one lock big,
  arrows, a swipe, dots, auto-advance `vtAutoNext` 0.9 s after a lock of mine opens), the locks
  (`vtLockHtml`), the notebook (`vtNotebookHtml`, `vtPageBody`, `vtRuleHtml`; the words of the
  notebook in the games' language, `VT_WORDS`; the lights' column of the current mistakes lit),
  «الكل»'s switch between the lock and the notebook (side by side on a laptop), the foot (chalk
  ✗, a hint, the chest's number), the safe's card and its result (`vtResHtml`, the board after
  each safe of a set), the end (`vtOverHtml`: the safes opened counting up and the room's best, or
  the podium and the board). `vtPaint` paints in place from small signatures (`vtBox`); one loop
  (`vtFrame`) burns the candles by the server's time (the smallest `receivedAt - serverNow`),
  flashes the lamp and counts down. `vtEvents` plays each event once per deal (stamps, haptics,
  the voice's sounds: the TV, else the host's phone). The TV (`vtTvHtml`): the attic
  (`vtAtticSvg`), the washing line (`vtLineHtml`: names and what they hold), the chest
  (`vtTvChestHtml`: lid, padlocks, glow, the chest's number), the candle and chalk board
  (`vtTvCandleHtml`), two chests and two candles for «فريقين», the stage drawn big when the TV
  opens, the card over the attic, toasts (`vtTvMoment`) and the lid opening.
- `roomTurnOf`: a lock still shut on this phone while the candle burns («الصندوق مستنيك!»).
- Tests: `rules.mjs` ("The vault": the numbers, pages and halves, seeds, the twist, the lights'
  rows, the start, the opener's and readers' slices, the card, strikes and the alarm, levels and
  the best, the symbols' order, «من الوقت», the lights after a mistake, a set's points and the
  night, endless banks nothing, «الكل», «فريقين», leaving, the TV opener and a phone taking over,
  a latecomer), `leaks.mjs` (`PROBES.vault`: exactly its own slice, a look only on its holder (or
  the screen when the TV opens), a page only on its phone, never a lock and its own page, no look,
  page or answer in `shared`; drivers `vault`, `vault:all`, `vault:teams`, `vault:tv`; proved by
  planting a page on the opener and the looks in `shared`), `play-all.mjs` (`--only=vault`: the
  robots open the safes from the opener's look and the readers' pages, a strike, the alarm,
  «الكل» as a set with a leaver, the night), `tools/validate-content.js` (300 notebooks and 2,100
  safes sound), `MAP` in `tools/test-changed.mjs`.
