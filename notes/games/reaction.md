# رد الفعل (id `reaction`)

The reaction test: one phone, wait for the green and tap. It had no notes file until 2 Oct 2026. Since 2 Oct 2026 it is also a room game, «أسرع إيد», inside the same card (the setup's «موبايل واحد / كل واحد بموبايله» switch, and one tile in a room's list).

## «خدعة» and «أسرع إيد» in rooms: built 2 Oct 2026 (the owner's answers of 2 Oct 2026)

Picked from the ideas page of 2 Oct 2026 (https://claude.ai/artifact/7Mhgw1ePSi3GhgEvDcSHxz, idea numbers in brackets) and every rule asked.

- **(391) «خدعة», fake signals**: about **one round in three** has a fake before the real green (a yellow flash, a 🐱, the word «استنى»). **A tap on a fake loses that round**: «اتضحك عليك!», counted as your worst time (✖ in a room). **A switch on the setup, on by default**, and it applies in rooms too.

- **(393) «أسرع إيد» in rooms**: **every phone and the TV turn green at the same server moment** (works with or without a TV), fair by the server's time as in الكراسي الموسيقية. **5 rounds; the fastest three each round get 3 / 2 / 1**, a tap before the green is ✖ for that round; a podium after the fifth, and the night's points from it.

### How it is built

**One phone (`JS_Reaction.html`, the `newgames` chunk; until 5 Oct 2026 in `JS_NewGames.html`).** `startReactionRound` / `handleReactionTap` / `showReactionResult` as before; the halves are `.rx-half` with a state class (`is-wait` red `--danger-btn`, `is-go` / `is-win` green `--success-btn`, `is-fake` / `is-tie` yellow `--pop`, `is-bad`, `is-idle` the night), painted by `rxPaintHalf` - the old `bg-red-500` / `bg-green-500` / `bg-yellow-500` / `text-white` are gone, every colour is a token with its own ink (`--*-on`). The fake: `rxDrawFake()` deals from a bag of three (`rxFakeBag`, one fake and two not, `shuffleArray`), so one round in three without a long run of none; a fake round waits 3.2-6.5 s (else 2-6 s), the fake comes 1.4 s in at the earliest and at least 1.1 s before the green, shows for `RX_FAKE_MS` (700 ms: `⚡` on yellow, a big `🐱`, or «استنى!» slamming in) and `appState.reaction.fakeSeen` is set; a tap after that is lost as «اتضحك عليك!» (`showReactionResult(..., fooled)`), before it as «بدري أوي!». The green pops (`motionBump`) and a win's time counts up (`countUp`). The switch on the setup (`#rx-fakes`, `rxSetFakes`, painted by `paintReactionSetup` from `SETUP_PAINTERS`) is `recallOptions('reaction', { fakes: true })`, the same option the room lobby's switch writes. `clearAllIntervals` and `rxStopRound` clear the fake's timers (`fakeId`, `fakeEndId`).

**The rules (`RoomReaction.js`, in `FILES` after `RoomChairs.js`; `reaction` / `REACTION_`).** `shared`: `settings { fakes }`, `roster` (the first twelve people; latecomers watch), `rounds` (5), `round`, `phase` ('wait' | 'go' | 'result' | 'gameover'), `startAt`, `greenAt` (only once green), `fake { kind, at, until }` (only while it shows) and `fakeSeen`, `taps` ([{ id, name, ms, foul: 'early' | 'fake' | null, arr }], fouls and no-taps last), `rows` (the round closed: + `pts`), `points`, `best`, `history` ([{ round, rows }]), `nextAt`, `board` ({ id, name, score, best, tie } best first, level points told apart by the best time). Secret: `room._reaction = { plan (the rounds with a fake: one or two of five, about one in three, drawn at the start), greenAt, fake { at, kind, shown } }` - never projected. Moves: `start` / `playAgain { fakes }` (the host; play again keeps the switch, points start again), `tap { round, at }` (once a round; in 'wait' it is ✖, 'fake' once a fake has shown; in 'go' the stamp is kept inside [green − `REACTION_GRACE_MS`, the arrival], never under `REACTION_MIN_REACT_MS` (100 ms) after the green, a stamp from before the green arriving after it is ✖), `nextRound { round }` (`requireMoveOn`, stale-guarded). The clock (`reactionDeadline` / `reactionTimeout`): a fake's start and end, the green (`greenAt` is the moment the server published it), the window's close (`REACTION_GO_MS`, 2.5 s, no tap = no time, not ✖), the next round (`REACTION_BETWEEN_MS`, 4.5 s). Everyone in (a time or ✖) closes the round at once; everyone ✖ before the green ends it red. Points 3 / 2 / 1 for the fastest three times (`REACTION_POINTS`); after round 5 the game is over and the night banks the board through `nightPlacesOf` like any game. `reactionPlayerLeft`: the leaver's tap goes, a round waiting only on them closes, fewer than two ends the game. Hooks in `RoomGames.js`: `applyRoomAction`, `roomDeadline`, `roomTimeout`, `gamePlayerLeft`, `clearGameState` (`room._reaction`).

**The page (`JS_RoomReaction.html`, its own chunk `reaction` in `tools/lazy-split.mjs`, which the one-phone `newgames` chunk loads with it; `rxr` / `RXR_`).** `ROOM_GAMES.reaction` (`lateJoin`, the lobby's «خدعة» switch, `startPayload { fakes }`) draws one big pad (`rxrPadHtml`, a `<button>` whose `pointerdown` is the tap, `rxrTap`), «داسوا: n من m» painted in place (so another's tap never rebuilds the pad under a finger), and after a round the rows (`rxrRowsHtml`: 🥇🥈🥉 or ✖ / —, the time, +3/+2/+1) and the totals (`renderScoreboard`, counted and slid by `animateScoreboards`), the next round's countdown, the host's «الجولة الجاية دلوقتي» (`roomMoveOnHtml`), and at the end the podium (`renderPodium`), confetti (`afterReveal`), play again / another game and the share button. The tap carries `rxrTapAt()` (Date.now() − the smallest `receivedAt − serverNow` seen, the chairs' `mchTapAt`) and is drawn at once (my time, or ✖), the server's verdict replacing it. The green's moment (`rxrAfter`, once a round): a ring of light from the pad's middle (`.rx-pad--go .rx-burst`), «دوس!» pops (`motionBump`), a buzz and the sound; my time counts up (`countUp`), a foul shakes the pad; the rows rise fastest last. `TV_GAMES.reaction`: the pad as the stage («دوس على موبايلك!»), everyone's ✓ / ✖ and points beside it, the rows and the totals after a round, the podium at the end; the host's buttons on the TV too. `roomTurnOf` says «خضرا: دوس!» (`turn_reaction`). `onRoomClocksReset` and `onLeaveScreen` stop the countdown.

### Decided here (open to change)

Each in one place in the code:

- **The room game's id is `reaction`**, the card's own (its help is the card's). Rooms of 2 to 12 people (`room: { min: 2 }`, the card now says 2-12 and shows the room and TV modes); no computer players; الشلة counts a first place toward «أسرع إيد» (`crew: 'fast'`).
- **"Counted as your worst time"** on one phone: a tap on a fake loses the round to the other half (there is no list of times on one phone); in a room it is ✖ for that round, no points.
- **Which fake**: one of the three kinds at random each time; one fake at most a round. One phone: a bag of three rounds; a room: one or two rounds of the five (`reactionFakePlan`, two with probability 2/3: on average 5/3, one in three).
- **A tap on red after a fake has shown is «اتضحك عليك!»** (`fakeSeen`), before any fake «بدري!».
- **No tap in 2.5 s after the green** is no time and no points, not ✖ (✖ is for a tap before the green only).
- **Level times** go by arrival; level points on the board by the best time of the game (`tie`).
- **The green is the moment the server publishes it** (not the planned moment), so a late alarm doesn't add to everyone's times; each phone beeps and buzzes itself as its screen turns green.
- **The round's result stays 4.5 s**, then the next round starts by itself; the host (or anyone, the host away) can start it sooner.
- **The yellow flash and a tie are `--pop`** (the arcade's amber, dark ink), the one yellow the tokens have in both themes; the TV's pad fills the stage.
